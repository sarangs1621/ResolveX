# Shipping, Orders & Returns Policy

## Order Tracking & Delivery
- Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days across North America and Europe.
- Expedited shipping delivers within 1-2 business days with live tracking provided via FedEx, UPS, or USPS.
- If your package shows as "in_transit", delivery is on schedule.
- If a delivery is delayed beyond 7 business days, customers qualify for complimentary expedited reshipment.

## 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee
- Any customer may request a 100% full refund on their order or software subscription within **14 calendar days** of the initial purchase date.
- Refunds inside 14 days are processed automatically back to the original credit card or bank account within 3-5 business days.

## Out-of-Window Returns (Over 14 Days)
- Requests received after 14 days cannot be processed automatically.
- Annual subscriptions may receive prorated store credit subject to human review.
- Hardware kits require inspection before credit is issued.
- If an order exceeds 14 days, the inquiry must be escalated to a senior support specialist.
