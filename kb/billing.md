# Billing & Plans

## Plans

- **Free**: 1 user, 100 requests/month, community support.
- **Pro**: $29/user/month, 10,000 requests/month, email support with 24h response time.
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing, unlimited requests, dedicated support, SSO, and a 99.9% uptime SLA. Contact sales@example.com.

## Payment

We accept all major credit cards and, on Enterprise plans, invoicing with NET-30 terms. Billing is monthly or annual; annual billing gets a 20% discount.

## Refunds

We offer a full refund within 14 days of any new subscription or upgrade, no questions asked. After 14 days, refunds are prorated for annual plans only and are handled case by case by the support team — these requests always require human review.

## Changing plans

You can upgrade at any time from Settings → Billing; the price difference is prorated immediately. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle.
